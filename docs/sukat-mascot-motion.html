How Sukat moves

7 looping samples, built from one rig with feet. Press Play on this board to see them move. Each one uses a classic animation principle.

The rig: 5 layers

Body
Case, clip, lock, bumper. Squashes and stretches from the bottom center.
Face
Eyes and mouth on their own layers, so they can blink and change without redrawing.
Blade arm
Pivots at the slot. Can rotate (wave, tap) or slide out (measure).
Feet
Two small rubber feet, separate from the body, like Duo’s. They step and stay planted when the body squashes.
Shadow
Shrinks and fades when the mascot jumps, so the jump feels real.

Idle

Slow in / slow out
Always on. Breathes, blinks every few seconds, blade sways a little.
Use for: Default state on any screen

Walk

Arcs + overlapping action
Little rubber feet step on a moving ruler. Body bobs, blade arm swings.
Use for: Onboarding, “Let’s go” moments, page changes

Wave

Anticipation + overshoot
Dips first, then waves the blade arm and settles back.
Use for: Sign in, chat welcome

Thinking

Secondary action
Taps the blade, looks up, dots pulse one by one.
Use for: “Reading receipt...”, AI answering

Happy jump

Squash and stretch
Squashes down, jumps and stretches, lands with a squash.
Use for: “Saved!”, scan done, goals hit

Confused

Timing (slow = unsure)
Rocks side to side, blade droops, “?” bobs.
Use for: “Please check this line”, empty scan

Measure (signature)

Follow-through
Pulls the tape out, shows a reading, then snaps it back with a little recoil.
Use for: Scanning, splash screen, logo intro
